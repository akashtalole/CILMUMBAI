# Source of this dataset

Copied unchanged from the Claude Impact Lab Mumbai challenge dataset "Follow the Rupee: Mumbai Monsoon Roads"
(github.com/akashtalole/Claude-Impact-Labs-data-2, commit 81d22806af98bded38bc15b0e7be248e4fbdfd3b), released under
CC BY 4.0. Everything in it is synthetic and every contractor is fictional; see README.md.

Every finding `hishob rupee` writes cites the file and line it came from, e.g. `work_orders.csv#L42`, where line 1
is the header.

`results/` holds what `hishob rupee build --claude` produced from it on 21 November 2026: the audit flags, every
complaint with its link and state, the ranked next-rupee plan per ward, and the scorecards with the notes Claude
wrote (each checked: every number is one of the computed figures, and no accusations).
